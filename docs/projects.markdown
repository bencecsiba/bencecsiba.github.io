I have most recently worked as part of the Deep Learning Team at AstraZeneca, focusing on volumetric segmentation of medical images and multimodal survival prediction.

I co-authored [LinGuinE](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/paper/3584_paper.pdf), a framework for longitudinal segmentation of tumours on medical scans. LinGuinE received early acceptance and was shortlisted for best paper at MICCAI 2026.
The repo can be found [here](https://github.com/n-e-garibli/LinGuinE/)
